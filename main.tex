\section{Introduction}
Digital payment services have become an important part of modern financial and commercial activities. The expansion of smartphones, internet access, mobile banking, digital wallets, and online shopping has made it possible for consumers to complete payments without relying entirely on physical cash. Digital payment services include several methods, such as debit and credit cards, mobile banking applications, electronic wallets, online payments, and contactless payment technologies. These services can make transactions faster and more convenient, while also providing users with electronic records of their financial activities.

The growth of digital payments has increased the importance of understanding how consumers perceive and evaluate these services. The availability of a digital payment method alone does not guarantee that consumers will use it regularly or feel satisfied with it. Satisfaction may depend on several characteristics of the service, including ease of use, security, reliability, convenience, transaction speed, and the quality of the supporting digital application.

Previous research has identified perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, actual usage, trust, interaction, service delivery, and security as relevant factors associated with electronic payment usage and customer satisfaction \cite{vinitha2018}.

Security and trust are particularly important because digital payment transactions involve financial and personal information. Consumers may be reluctant to use a payment service if they are concerned about fraud, unauthorized access, privacy, or transaction errors.

Convenience and ease of use are also central to the digital payment experience. Consumers generally expect payment systems to reduce the time and effort required to complete transactions. Features such as simple interfaces, quick confirmation, mobile access, contactless payment, and transaction histories may therefore contribute to a more positive experience.

Age may also influence how consumers perceive and use digital payment technologies. Younger and older consumers can differ in their familiarity with smartphones and digital applications, their experience with online services, and their preferences for traditional versus digital payment methods. Therefore, examining age may help explain whether the relationship between digital payment services and consumer satisfaction is similar across different age groups.

This study focuses on the impact of digital payment services on consumer satisfaction. In particular, it considers the role of ease of use, security and reliability, convenience and efficiency, contactless payment, digital wallets and mobile applications, transaction records, and the reduction of reliance on cash and paperwork. The study also considers whether age influences the relationship between digital payment services and consumer satisfaction.

\section{Research Problem}

Although digital payment services offer several potential advantages, not all users experience the same level of satisfaction. Problems related to security, reliability, complexity, technical failures, or lack of trust may reduce users' willingness to depend on digital payment systems.

Previous studies have examined individual factors affecting satisfaction, but there is still a need to understand how several characteristics of digital payment services work together from the consumer's perspective.

In addition, age may influence how consumers perceive and use digital payment technologies. Understanding this issue can help payment providers and financial institutions design services that are easier, safer, and more suitable for different groups of consumers.

\section{Research Objectives}

The study aims to:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Examine the impact of digital payment services on consumer satisfaction.
    \item Determine whether ease of use is associated with consumer satisfaction.
    \item Examine the role of security and reliability in consumer satisfaction.
    \item Assess the effect of convenience and efficiency on consumer satisfaction.
    \item Examine the relationship between digital wallets and mobile applications and consumer satisfaction.
    \item Investigate whether transaction records and reduced dependence on cash contribute to satisfaction.
    \item Examine whether age influences the relationship between digital payment services and consumer satisfaction.
\end{enumerate}
